# Decision Log — prd-DarkLab-auto-corrections

- **2026-11-11 — Auto corrections write standard Develop Settings keys:** Instead of applying an uneditable black-box LUT, Auto Tone, Auto WB, Auto Upright, and Auto Lens write directly to `Exposure`, `Highlights`, `Shadows`, `Whites`, `Blacks`, `Temp`, `Tint`, `CropAngle`, `TransformVertical`, etc., so every automatic decision is transparent and tweakable by the Photographer.
- **2026-11-11 — Unified with Epic 21 (Auto Upright):** Epic 25 incorporates the geometry algorithms and UI bindings needed for Auto Upright and extends them with Auto Tone, Auto WB, Auto Presence, and Auto Lens.
