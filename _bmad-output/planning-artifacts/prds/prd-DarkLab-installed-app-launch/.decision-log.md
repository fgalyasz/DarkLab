# Decision log — prd-DarkLab-installed-app-launch

## 2026-11-08 — Collect pyexiv2 binaries in PyInstaller

`pyexiv2/lib/__init__.py` loads `libexiv2.dylib` dynamically via `ctypes.CDLL(os.path.join(lib_dir, 'libexiv2.dylib'))` before importing `exiv2api.so`. Without `--collect-binaries pyexiv2`, PyInstaller only sees `exiv2api.so` and skips `libexiv2.dylib` and its Homebrew dependencies (`libINIReader`, `libinih`, `libintl`, `libbrotli*`). Passing `--collect-binaries pyexiv2` bundles `libexiv2.dylib` and rewrites its dependent load commands into `Contents/Frameworks/`.

## 2026-11-08 — Store logs under ~/Library/Logs/DarkLab

Finder launches `/Applications/DarkLab.app` with working directory `/`, and `__file__` inside a frozen bundle resolves under `/Applications/DarkLab.app/Contents/Frameworks/`. Writing `darklab.log`, `import_dialog.log`, or `library_panel.log` to relative or `__file__`-relative paths either raises `OSError: [Errno 30] Read-only file system` or mutates the signed `.app` bundle. `src/startup.py` provides `log_directory()` and `log_file()` rooted at `~/Library/Logs/DarkLab`.

Fast path. New FR-76. Story #100 under Epic #71.
