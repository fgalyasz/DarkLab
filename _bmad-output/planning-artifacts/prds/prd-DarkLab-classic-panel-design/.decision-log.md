# Decision Log — Classic Darkroom Panel Design

## 2026-11-27 — Supersede rounded 10px card chrome with sharp flat classic panels

The founder shared a classic darkroom Develop workspace screenshot and requested DarkLab's panels match its colors, slider pointers, object spacings, and dividers. This supersedes `prd-DarkLab-uniform-panels` (`PANEL_CORNER_RADIUS_PX = 10` and glossy silver headers) in favor of `PANEL_CORNER_RADIUS_PX = 0`, flat matte charcoal headers (`rgb(50, 50, 50)`), neutral gray panel bodies (`rgb(80, 80, 80)`), and etched bevel dividers.

## 2026-11-27 — Preserve NFR-7 contrast (`>= 4.5:1`) on `#505050` surfaces

In the founder's reference screenshot, the photo is offline/missing so Basic controls appear in a disabled dark-gray state while active controls (`Presets`, panel headers) use light gray text. DarkLab renders active body text in `rgb(224, 224, 224)` (`6.11:1` on `rgb(80, 80, 80)` and `9.71:1` on `rgb(50, 50, 50)`), satisfying `NFR-7`, while `:disabled` controls drop to muted dark gray `rgb(42, 42, 42)`.

## 2026-11-27 — Keep `DevelopGroup` collapsible while rendering flat inline headers

Existing density tests and keyboard/mouse workflows rely on `DevelopGroup` sections (`Treatment`, `WB`, `Tone`, `Presence`) remaining collapsible. Styling `DevelopGroup` with a flat inline heading and top etched divider delivers the seamless classic panel look without removing collapse capability.

## 2026-11-27 — Custom QPainter pentagon slider pointer with upper tick marks

Qt stylesheets cannot express an upward-pointing pentagon pointer aligned with upper graduated tick marks cleanly across macOS/Windows/Linux HiDPI screens. Implementing `paintEvent` on `DevelopSlider` with pure geometry helpers gives crisp, resolution-independent rendering and unit-testable pointer/tick coordinate math.
